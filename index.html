Write in Lio
An organic typeface

Lio typeface designed by Lidia & Oli

every letter

grows from

one line

Letters that grow like architecture.

Lio is an organic typeface by Lidia and Oli. It takes its forms from Antoni Gaudí and Catalan Modernisme: from leaves cast in iron, from arches found by hanging chains, from the broken tiles of trencadís.

Every glyph grows from a single continuous thread, either up like the sky, along like grass, or down like roots.

01 — Direction

Sky, grass, root

Every letter of the alphabet belongs to one of three directions. Together they decide where the line goes next, so a word reads like a plant growing across the page.

02 — Alphabet

Twenty-six seeds

Lio is a lowercase alphabet. Each glyph meets its neighbours on a shared thread, so a word is drawn without lifting the pen. Choose a letter to look at it closely.

03 — Parameters

Four rules, one line

The typeface began as a page of sketches and four questions. Every glyph answers all of them.

  1. 01

    Direction

    Where does the letter grow? Up to the sky, along the grass or down to the root.

    dog
  2. 02

    Structure

    A horizontal thread runs through every glyph, the stem that each form grows from.

    stem
  3. 03

    Complexity

    A handful of shapes (bud, leaf, thorn, tendril) repeated and varied, like ornament.

    leaf
  4. 04

    Terminal

    Strokes never end bluntly. They taper and curl the way a tendril does.

04 — Origins

Structure becomes letter

Catalan Modernisme treated structure as ornament and ornament as structure. Gaudí found his geometry in trees, bones and shells, and the poets of the time wrote the land, the sea and the light into the language. Lio borrows the same habit. Here is each building next to the letters it became.

05 — Sound

Letters you can hear

Read from left to right, every Lio glyph behaves like a sound wave: a lens swells and fades, a hook is a gust that turns back, a thorn is the instant of an impact, a fork is a split. Each letter is paired with a natural sound made of its own material. Sky letters are air, grass letters are water, leaves and seeds, and root letters are stone, earth and wood. Press any letter to hear it.

06 — Poems

Written to be grown

Short poems written for Lio, using only the letters it knows. Hover over or focus a line to read it, or show every reading at once.

07 — Write

Grow your own words

Type anything. Lio folds accents into their base letters (à, ç, ñ) and keeps a–z, spaces and line breaks.
